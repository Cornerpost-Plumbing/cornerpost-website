# Cornerpost Standard

**Standard ID:** WEB-001
**Standard Name:** Website Design Standard
**Approval Status:** Approved
**Revision:** 1.0

## Purpose

This standard defines the long-term design philosophy for every public-facing Cornerpost Plumbing website. It establishes the principles that should guide future website decisions regardless of technology.

## Scope

Applies to all current and future Cornerpost Plumbing websites, landing pages, and public web content.

## Design Intent

The Cornerpost website exists to build trust before it generates leads.

Every page, image, sentence, button, and interaction should increase confidence that Cornerpost Plumbing is a professional company that performs thoughtful, high-quality work.

The website should communicate professionalism, competence, honesty, craftsmanship, reliability, transparency, and calm confidence.

## Standard Requirements

1. Trust Before Conversion
- Trust is the primary objective.
- Calls to action shall never come at the expense of credibility.

2. Education Before Selling
- Explain services, problems solved, expectations, when to call, and why recommendations are made.
- Reduce uncertainty rather than create it.

3. Honest Communication
- Accurate
- Respectful
- Concise
- Technically correct
- Understandable by homeowners

Avoid exaggerated claims, fear-based marketing, artificial urgency, unnecessary superlatives, and empty buzzwords.

4. Timeless Design
Use clean layouts, generous spacing, restrained colors, consistent typography, high readability, and purposeful photography.

Avoid trendy effects, unnecessary animation, clutter, and decorative elements without purpose.

5. Purposeful Simplicity
Every page and every section shall have a clear purpose.

6. Consistency
Remain consistent with branding, printed materials, estimates, invoices, uniforms, vehicle graphics, customer communications, and the Cornerpost Service System.

7. Accessibility
Remain easy to use on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

8. Authentic Representation
Prefer actual work, equipment, employees, and service vehicles over stock photography whenever practical.

9. Long-Term Maintainability
Favor designs that remain understandable and maintainable by future developers.

## Implementation Guidance

When multiple acceptable design choices exist, select the one that best improves customer trust, clarity, consistency, and long-term maintainability.

## Exceptions

Document any intentional deviation from this standard.

## Philosophy

The Cornerpost website should never feel like marketing. It should feel like the digital extension of the company itself.

If a design decision increases trust, clarity, and long-term consistency, it is probably the correct decision.

If it primarily attracts attention without improving confidence, it should be reconsidered.
